PRISM is my work on instruction-following for Punjabi text rewriting at TIET.
I worked on the dataset pipeline, model fine-tuning, and evaluation.

## Building the data

I collected 16.43 million tokens from more than 600 web sources, then used Gemini
2.0 Flash to generate instruction-response examples for six rewriting tasks.
The dataset contains more than 91,000 examples.

Generated examples needed validation before training. Structural validation removed 37% of the generated samples.

## Training and evaluation

I fully fine-tuned LLaMA 3.1 8B on A100 GPUs with Unsloth, using English instructions
and Punjabi outputs in Alpaca format.

I compared the model with LLaMA 3.1 Instruct, Mistral 7B, Gemma 7B, and Phi-3 Mini
across six tasks. Evaluation used BLEU, chrF++, ROUGE-L, BERTScore-F1, and Word-F1.
These measure different kinds of similarity to a reference answer; they don't
replace a person checking whether a rewrite preserves the intended meaning.
