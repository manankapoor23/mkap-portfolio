## Two failure modes

Dense retrieval matches meaning but misses exact tokens — product codes, error
strings, rare names. BM25 matches those tokens but misses paraphrase. They fail on
different queries, which is exactly why combining them works.

## Combining them

Run both, then fuse the rankings — reciprocal rank fusion is a simple, strong
default that needs no score calibration between the two systems. The dense side
recovers "how do I cancel" ≈ "stopping a subscription"; the sparse side nails
"ERR_4032".

## Grounding is the point

Retrieval only matters if generation is actually constrained to what came back.
In the support-triage agent, answers are hard-grounded in retrieved passages with
zero external lookups — retrieval isn't a suggestion to the model, it's the boundary.
