## The base model can't stay in-script

Ask base LLaMA 3.1 8B to rewrite Gurmukhi and it drifts to Devanagari or emits
character-level noise. That's a data problem, not a capacity problem — there was no
task-conditioned Punjabi instruction data to learn from.

## Teacher generation

A Gemini 2.0 Flash teacher generated instruction–response pairs across six rewriting
tasks, seeded from 16.43M tokens across 600+ sources. The interesting work is after
generation: structural validation to drop malformed samples before they poison training.

## Evaluating honestly

Aggregate metrics hide per-task collapse. Benchmarking against four baselines across
five automatic metrics — measured per task — is what makes "it works" a claim you can
actually defend.
